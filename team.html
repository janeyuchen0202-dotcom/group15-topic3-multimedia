Peer-review checklist

  • ✓Every technical statement matches the source it cites, and calculated numbers are marked as ours.
  • ✓At least five annotated resources, including one standard or peer-reviewed paper with a DOI or stable official URL.
  • ✓An original diagram with a credit line, and a comparison table with its sources.
  • ✓At least two applications with citations, and at least two contextual links to other sections that explain the relationship.
  • ✓A reflection of 100–150 words in the owner’s own words, key points, one misconception and one verified claim with its exact location.
  • ✓An accurate AI and source use statement, and every link opens in a private browser window.

How the sources were checked

  • ✓Every DOI was resolved through Crossref, and its title, authors, year and venue were compared with our citation.
  • ✓Every web link was opened on 25 Nov 2026. The OpenCV documentation blocks automated checks, so its wording was confirmed in the official source files in the OpenCV GitHub repository that generate those pages.
  • ✓Specific numbers (sampling rates, bit depths, coefficients, bandwidths, timings) were checked in the standard, paper or document they are attributed to. Numbers we calculated are labelled as our calculations.
  • ✓Each section ends with a verified claim that names the exact clause, section or page where the evidence is.
  • ✓Candidate sources that could not be opened and read were left out rather than cited from memory.

AI and source use: group statement

Tools used. Claude (Anthropic) drafted the page outlines, explanatory text, comparison tables and the code for the six diagrams and the learning map, and ran the citation and link checks described above.

How the output was treated. AI output was treated as a draft, not as a source. Every technical claim on this site is attributed to a paper, standard or official document listed in the resource library, and each page owner reviewed and edited their own page. Individual statements appear at the end of each section page.
